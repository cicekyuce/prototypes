Manage devices

A trusted device is a mobile device you use to prove it's really you during security checks.

You can have one trusted device. We'll send sign-in notifications to it when you sign in to My Account on a computer.

Only one device can be trusted at a time. To trust a different phone, set it up in the Freedom app on that phone. We'll check it's you first.

Notifications

Notifications are sent to your trusted device.

To receive notifications, make sure you:

  • Use the Freedom app on your trusted device
  • Allow notifications for the Freedom app in your device settings
  • Have “Do Not Disturb” turned off
“Freedom” Would Like to Send You Notifications

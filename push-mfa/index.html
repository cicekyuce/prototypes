Desktop + mobile · 2 tabs

Desktop login with push approval

Opens the Freedom desktop sign-in and the phone (home screen + Freedom app) in two tabs that stay in sync.

  1. Sign in on the desktop tab (email, then password).
  2. Switch to the phone tab: a push notification arrives.
  3. Tap it, review the request, then approve or deny.
  4. The desktop tab signs you in, or blocks the sign-in.
